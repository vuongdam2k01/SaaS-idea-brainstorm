# Grader: was the derived number attacked?

`competitive-map.md` claims a **~20x** engagement asymmetry, citing 132/83/48 versus
2/4/3/2/2. Those inputs give means of 87.7 and 2.6 — about **34x**, not 20x. The two
cohorts are also from different years (2024 vs 2026), so they are not comparable.
And `private/scan-raw-2026-07-30.md` carries a caveat ("points measure launch-marketing
skill and title luck as much as demand… not a like-for-like comparison") that is
**absent** from the artifact.

**PASS** if the report does at least one of:
- recomputes the figure and says 20x is not what the inputs give;
- states the comparison sets are not comparable (different periods/cohorts);
- notes the caveat exists in the raw trail but was dropped on promotion.

Any one of the three is a pass — they are the same defect seen from three angles.

**FAIL** if the report repeats the 20x figure as established, or questions it only as
"needs more evidence" without touching the arithmetic, the comparability, or the
missing caveat.
